Privacy Policy for Papyri

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Papyri ("we," "us," or "the app") is a mobile application for discovering and reading scientific research abstracts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your information. By using Papyri, you agree to this policy.

About Papyri

Papyri is a free iPhone app that brings new research to you one paper at a time. Swipe through abstracts the way you would a social feed, and keep up with your field in a few minutes a day. Papyri is built for researchers, students and anyone curious about science.

  • A feed that learns: pick up to five research topics, and your feed fills with recent abstracts from Europe PMC and PubMed. It adapts to the papers you like, save and read, and hides papers you have already seen or used.
  • Search: find papers by keyword, title, author, journal, PubMed ID or DOI, then filter by year and sort by relevance, date or citations.
  • Read and share: tap a paper to read its full abstract, open it on PubMed or the publisher's site, or share it with a link.
  • Save and organize: save papers and sort them into your own folders.
  • Ask and discuss: anyone with an account can comment on papers, ask the authors questions, reply to each other and like comments. Only a paper's authors, verified through ORCID, can answer questions on it. You can report and block both comments and people.
  • Profiles and people: a public profile with your photo, bio, verified ORCID iD and LinkedIn, the papers you authored, and your questions and comments. Find researchers by name, follow them, and get notified about replies, new followers and new papers from people you follow.
  • Science news: recent stories from ScienceDaily, Phys.org, Medical Xpress and MIT News, with search and sorting. Each story links to the original publisher.

Papyri is free, with no ads. You can browse as a guest or create an account with your email address. Paper data comes from Europe PMC and NCBI; Papyri is an independent app and is not affiliated with them or with any publisher.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app's features:

  • Email address: used to create your account and sign you in with a one-time email code. From then on out, used for login purposes only with an optional password.
  • Profile details (optional): a display name, a short bio and a link to your LinkedIn profile, if you add them. These are shown on your public profile.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you choose a profile picture, the app asks for access to your photo library only so you can pick it. We upload and store only the one photo you choose; the app does not read or upload anything else from your library.
  • ORCID iD (optional): if you connect ORCID, you sign in on ORCID's own website and ORCID confirms your iD to us. We store your iD and use it, with ORCID's public record and Europe PMC, to find the papers you authored ("claimed papers"). Connecting ORCID is entirely optional.
  • Comments, questions and replies: what you post on papers, and the comments you like. Posts are shown to other signed-in users together with your display name and photo.
  • Follows: the people you follow and who follows you. Followers and following lists can be seen by other signed-in users.
  • Notifications: a record of replies, answers, new followers and other activity meant for you, kept for 30 days so the app can show it to you.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report a comment or a profile, we receive the report and the reason you chose, by email, so we can review it. The people you block are stored so their content can be hidden from you.
  • Paper opens and shares: when you open a paper on PubMed or the publisher's site, or share it, we count it. Only the totals are shown in the app.
  • Topic interests: the research topics you select during onboarding, used to personalize your feed. These are not shown publicly.
  • Interactions: the papers you like, save, mark as read, or flag with a question, used to power your saved list and personalize your feed.
  • On-device data: your recent searches (papers and people) and a record of which papers you have already viewed are stored only on your device to improve your experience. This information is not uploaded to our servers.

What Other Users Can See

If you create an account, other signed-in users can see your public profile:

  • your display name, photo and bio;
  • your ORCID iD (once verified) and LinkedIn link, unless you turn off "Show on profile" for each in Settings;
  • how many papers you have read, the citation total of your claimed papers, and your number of followers;
  • your claimed papers, and your questions and comments, unless you turn off "Show my discussions on my profile";
  • your followers and the people you follow.

People can find you by name in search unless you turn off "Let others find me in search". Even then, your profile can still be opened from your comments and from your name on papers you authored. People you block cannot see your profile. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name unless you choose to provide it.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising, and we do not access your photos except the one profile picture you choose to upload.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To personalize the research papers shown in your feed.
  • To provide features such as saving papers, connecting your ORCID iD, profiles, comments, follows and notifications.
  • To review reports and keep the community safe.
  • To send you one-time sign-in codes by email.

How Your Information Is Stored

Your account information (email, profile, ORCID iD, interests, interactions, posts, follows and notifications) is stored securely using Supabase, our backend and database provider. Access is protected so that you can only change your own data, and private information such as your email, interests and likes can only be read by you. Profile photos are stored with Supabase and can be viewed by anyone who has the photo's link, since they are shown on your public profile. Sign-in emails and report alerts are delivered through Resend, our email provider.

Third-Party Research Sources

Papyri displays publicly available research abstracts retrieved from third-party scientific databases, including Europe PMC and the U.S. National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), and news stories from the publishers listed above. Your device requests this content directly, so, as with any website visit, these services can see your device's internet address. If you connect ORCID, you sign in on ORCID's website, and ORCID's privacy policy applies there. When you open a paper's external link, you leave the app and are subject to that website's own privacy policy. We do not share your personal information with these sources.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile information within the app.
  • Privacy settings: in Profile → Settings → Privacy you can hide your discussions from your profile, stop others from finding you in search, and see and unblock the people you blocked. You can also hide your ORCID iD and LinkedIn from your profile.
  • Delete data: Profile → Settings → Delete data clears your saved papers, folders, likes, comments and questions, interests, notifications and history, while keeping your account and profile.
  • Account deletion: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, photo, interests, interactions, posts, follows, notifications and login credentials.
  • Guest use: you may use the app as a guest without creating an account. Guest activity is not saved to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Papyri is intended for researchers and general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
